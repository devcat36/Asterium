# Stellarium's backers & sponsors

This is upstream Stellarium's list of the people and organisations who funded
it, reproduced here because they funded the work this fork is built on and the
credit is owed to them. Asterium neither solicits nor receives any of it. The
page linked below is the Stellarium project's, not this fork's; giving there
supports Stellarium, which is the intended effect.

Since Feb 2019, the Stellarium team accept donations through their [Open Collective page](https://opencollective.com/stellarium).
The money will be used transparently to pay for development, servers and other project-related costs.

The Stellarium team gives a huge thanks to all individual one-time supporters, regular backers and our sponsors:
